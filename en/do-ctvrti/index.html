01 The house

A house for six households. Ground floor with gardens, first floor with balconies, roof with terraces. Your own lift down to the garage and neighbours you know by name.

Soft curves,strong character.

The corner of Do čtvrti and Malá Tyršovka streets. A set-back top floor, rounded corners and balustrades of perforated brick that let in light while keeping privacy.

Visualisations by JA Architekti. Interior furnishings are illustrative.

A garden behind the house, not behind the city.

Ground-floor apartments have their own gardens of 84 and 91 m², the first floor has balconies and the top apartments roof terraces of over 40 m². Living rooms face south, into the greenery.

Light through brick.

Perforated brickwork on the balustrades and in the entrance hall lets light through while protecting privacy. Inside: warm-toned plasters, wood and stone.

    03 Pricing

    Reserve with an advantage.

    We sell the apartments during the building permit procedure. Decide now and you get a discount on the apartment price according to the size of your deposit. The deposit counts towards the purchase price.

    Apartment prices exclude the garage space and cellar, which can be purchased separately. Discounted prices are rounded to thousands of CZK. We will send you the price list of apartments, garage spaces and cellars on request, just fill in the form below. The discount is calculated from the apartment price. The apartment area is the floor area under Czech Government Regulation No. 366/2013 Coll. (an indicative estimate from the plans); the binding figure will be stated in the declaration of the owner.

    1. You choose an apartment and pay a deposit

      CZK 3, 4 or 5 million, depending on the discount you want. The deposit is part of the purchase price.

    2. The discount is fixed in the contract

      A discount of 3, 4 or 5 % of the apartment price is written into the reservation agreement and then the purchase agreement.

    3. Your deposit is safe

      If the building permit does not become final by 31 October 2028, we refund your deposit in full. How the deposit is held is set out in the reservation agreement.
