01 The house

Every apartment has its own garden or terrace. Materials that age beautifully: exposed concrete, wood and glass. And a house that makes its own energy.

A house you wantto see up close.

Three storeys with stepped terraces, green roofs and a patinated metal facade that changes colour with the light.

Visualisations by JA Architekti. Interior furnishings are illustrative.

The terrace as another room.

Ground-floor apartments have their own garden, upper floors have terraces from 25 to 104 m². Timber decking, green roofs and glass balustrades. You will spend more time outside than you expected.

Materials that age beautifully.

A facade of metal panels with exposed joints changes colour over the day. Inside: exposed concrete, three-layer wooden floors and mineral plasters. Nothing that would need redoing in a few years.

Privacy first.

Apartments are entered from open galleries on the north side. Living rooms and terraces face south, into the garden. A reinforced-concrete wall between apartments means you will not hear the neighbours.

    03 Pricing

    Reserve with an advantage.

    We sell the apartments during the building permit procedure. Decide now and you get a discount on the apartment price according to the size of your deposit. The deposit counts towards the purchase price.

    Apartment prices exclude the garage space and cellar, which can be purchased separately. Discounted prices are rounded to thousands of CZK. We will send you the price list of apartments, garage spaces and cellars on request, just fill in the form below. The discount is calculated from the apartment price. The apartment area is an estimate of the floor area under Czech Government Regulation No. 366/2013 Coll.; the binding figure will be stated in the declaration of the owner.

    1. You choose an apartment and pay a deposit

      CZK 3, 4 or 5 million, depending on the discount you want. The deposit is part of the purchase price.

    2. The discount is fixed in the contract

      A discount of 3, 4 or 5 % of the apartment price is written into the reservation agreement and then the purchase agreement.

    3. Your deposit is safe

      If the building permit does not become final by 31 October 2028, we refund your deposit in full. How the deposit is held is set out in the reservation agreement.

    04 Standard

    A house that saves energy.

    Energy

    • Cascade of air-to-water heat pumps
    • Rooftop photovoltaic plant with battery storage
    • Underfloor heating and cooling
    • No gas connection

    Structure and interior

    • Reinforced-concrete structure, exposed concrete
    • Three-layer wooden floors, mineral plasters
    • Large-format glazing, aluminium windows
    • Party walls Rw 57–58 dB

    The house

    • Lift from the garage to every floor
    • 16 garage spaces, 8 cellars
    • Green roofs and a shared roof terrace
    • Pre-wired for electric-car charging

    Based on the building permit documentation (12/2025). Final execution, layouts and areas may change.
