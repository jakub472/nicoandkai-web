Privacypolicy.

We appreciate your interest in our houses and value your privacy just as much. Here you can find what data we process, why, and what your rights are.

1. Who processes your data

The controller of personal data is Nico & Kai Development, Company ID , registered office (“we”). For Rezidence ke Schodům the seller is Rezidence Ke schodům s.r.o., Company ID 23662956, Ke schodům 1465/14, 143 00 Prague 12 – Modřany, to whom we pass on your enquiry.

Contact for personal data matters: .

2. What data we process

  • Form data: name and surname, e-mail, phone (if you provide it), the text of your message, the selected apartment and interest in a garage space or cellar.
  • Technical data: when the website loads, the hosting provider processes your IP address and browser details in the server logs.

The website uses no tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Your browser only remembers for the duration of your visit that you have seen the intro animation (a technical necessity, no personal data).

3. Why we process data and on what basis

  • Answering your enquiry and negotiating an apartment reservation – steps prior to entering into a contract at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Sending information about the project you are interested in (construction news, price list, dates) – based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Operation and security of the website – our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. How long we keep data

For the duration of negotiations about the reservation and purchase of an apartment and then for no longer than 3 years after the last contact, or until consent is withdrawn, whichever comes first. Data that forms part of a contract is kept for the period required by law.

5. Who we share data with

  • The seller of the given project (see point 1), so that it can negotiate the reservation with you.
  • For Apartmány Vysoké, the estate agency Svoboda & Williams, which sells the last units.
  • The website hosting provider (GitHub, Inc., USA – server logs; the transfer is covered by EU standard contractual clauses) and the e-mail service provider.

We never sell data or pass it on for third-party marketing.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time (withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal). Just write to us. If you believe we process your data unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, uoou.gov.cz.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when hosting or tools change. The current version is always on this page.

Effective from 21 November 2026.
