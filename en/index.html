03 How we build

Simply,honestly, to last.

Every apartment gets a piece of sky.

A garden, a terrace or a balcony. We do not build houses where the outdoors is just for parking. Living rooms face south, into the greenery, and we count the outdoor space as another room.

Materials that age beautifully.

Exposed concrete, wood, warm-toned plasters, brick and glass. Fewer elements, but better ones. Nothing that has to be replaced in a few years.

Trust is built like a house.

Clear information, clear dates and direct contact with the people behind the project. If something changes, you hear it from us first.
